Overview

Practical, Skill-Focused Education

The Kenyan Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) represents a modern, learner-centric educational shift designed to nurture specific core competencies rather than relying purely on rote memorization. At Redemption International School, we implement CBC with a focus on empowering students to apply what they learn to real-life situations.

By encouraging active participation, collaboration, values-based behavior, and parental involvement, the CBC pathway shapes children into self-reliant, ethical, and highly productive citizens. Our curriculum integrates advanced STEM elements and language training, giving learners a well-rounded and globally competent foundation.

Core Competencies

CBC Outcomes

The Kenyan CBC framework is built upon 7 key competencies designed to equip learners with vital 21st-century skills.

Communication & Collaboration

Teaching students to express ideas clearly and work harmoniously in diverse teams.

Self-Efficacy

Building confidence, responsibility, and the capacity to initiate and complete tasks independently.

Critical Thinking & Problem Solving

Equipping learners to analyze information, make logical decisions, and solve real-world challenges.

Creativity & Imagination

Encouraging artistic expression, innovative design, and out-of-the-box conceptual thinking.

Citizenship

Instilling patriotism, civic responsibility, respect for diverse cultures, and positive community engagement.

Learning to Learn

Cultivating a lifelong passion for knowledge, curiosity, and continuous self-improvement.

Digital Literacy

Training learners to navigate technology safely, innovatively, and productively for research and expression.
