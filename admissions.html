Admissions Process

Begin your child's journey at Redemption International School. We are excited to welcome new families to our growing community.

1

Access the Application Form

The application form is available for download below. Please download and print the form before proceeding to fill it in.

Download Application Form
2

Complete the Form

Ensure all sections are filled accurately, including pupil details, home address, parental information, and medical history.

3

Attach Required Documents

  • Copy of birth certificate or passport
  • Recent passport-sized photographs
  • Previous academic reports
  • Relevant medical reports (if applicable)
4

Submit the Application

Submit via email to info@redemptioninternationalschool.com or in-person at the school office in Munyonyo.
