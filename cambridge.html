Overview

Globally Recognized Academic Excellence

Redemption International School is proud to offer the Cambridge International Curriculum, a world-class academic program created by Cambridge Assessment International Education (a part of the University of Cambridge). It is designed to inspire a lifelong passion for learning and equip students with critical-thinking, problem-solving, and research skills.

Our Cambridge pathway is structured to guide learners seamlessly from age 5 through to university prep. By combining the global perspective of Cambridge with the values-based culture of RIS, we ensure that students are not only academically superior but also socially responsible and culturally aware.

Educational Journey

Cambridge Pathways

A structured, flexible, and fully integrated curriculum designed to guide learners to achieve their potential.

Cambridge Primary

Ages 5–11 – Fostering strong foundations in English, Mathematics, Science, and Digital Literacy.

Cambridge Lower Secondary

Ages 11–14 – Transitioning learners into broader academic topics and independent research skills.

Cambridge IGCSE

Ages 14–16 – Globally recognized qualifications with diverse subject choices and rigorous testing.

Advanced (A-Levels)

Ages 16–19 – Deep pre-university training that prepares students for top universities worldwide.
