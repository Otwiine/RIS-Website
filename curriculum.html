Our Curriculum

Christian‑Based, Individualized Learning

Accelerated Christian Education (ACE) blends faith, character, and academics for every learner.

ACE Curriculum Overview

Our ACE program nurtures spiritual growth, self‑paced study, and mastery of core subjects from early childhood through secondary education.

Learning Stages

RIS Day Care

Infants & toddlers – communication, motor skills, early socialisation.

Step By Step (3.5‑4 yrs)

Foundational phonics, shapes, colours, fine‑motor development.

Pre‑School (Grade R)

Storytelling, songs, reading preparation, basic numeracy.

ABC’s / Grade One

Phonics, writing, numbers, responsibility, faith‑based values.

Intermediate Class

Independent learning, skill building, personal space, moral growth.

Upper Learning Center

Advanced academics, presentations, university prep, goal‑setting.

Robotics & STEM Integration

Our state‑of‑the‑art robotics lab empowers students to design, build, and program. The program cultivates problem‑solving, teamwork, and future‑ready tech skills.

Explore Robotics Program

Multilingual Education

At RIS, we believe in a global perspective. Our comprehensive language program includes German, French, Spanish, and Kiswahili, preparing our learners to thrive in an interconnected world.
