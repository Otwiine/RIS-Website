Tailored Educational Pathways

Nurturing Unique Talents and Strengths

At Redemption International School, we recognize that every student has unique learning styles, strengths, and goals. We offer three distinct educational pathways to help learners thrive academically, spiritually, and practically in a dynamic global environment.

Accelerated Christian Education (ACE)

A Bible-based, individualized, and self-paced curriculum that nurtures spiritual growth, academic mastery, and Christian character from early childhood to secondary levels.

Explore ACE

Cambridge International

A world-renowned curriculum designed to challenge students, foster intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and international-mindedness, preparing them for top global universities.

Explore Cambridge

Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC)

The Kenyan national pathway focusing on practical competencies, creative talents, and life skills, preparing students for self-reliance and civic responsibility.

Explore CBC

Robotics & STEM Integration

Our state‑of‑the‑art robotics lab empowers students to design, build, and program. The program cultivates problem‑solving, teamwork, and future‑ready tech skills.

Explore Robotics Program

Multilingual Education

At RIS, we believe in a global perspective. Our comprehensive language program includes German, French, Spanish, and Kiswahili, preparing our learners to thrive in an interconnected world.

Explore Languages Program
