About RIS

Fostering learning, growth, and lifelong success.

Redemption International School was established on September 23, 2019, beginning with ten students and six committed parents who recognized the need to close gaps in their children's development and sought a more effective approach to education. While international education had grown in popularity, many essential elements were still missing. RIS was founded to bridge these gaps and offer a well-rounded, future-ready learning experience.

Education is a shared responsibility, and we believe parents play a vital role in nurturing talent, building discipline, and supporting academic growth. At RIS, we implement the ACE curriculum—a Bible-based program focused on developing the whole child. In addition to strong academics, we provide multilingual education in German, French, Spanish, and Kiswahili, alongside programs in AI, robotics, coding, and programming, all aimed at preparing our learners to thrive in a rapidly evolving world.

Why choose us?

Teaching children how to live not just how to make a living

A unique place where your child will experience small class sizes, an engaging, Bible-based K-12 curriculum with a global perspective, and personalized attention. Conveniently located at the Munyonyo Roundabout with easy access via the Bypass and Entebbe Expressway, we proudly serve communities as far as Ntinda, Gayaza, Kigo, Munyonyo, Buziga, Makindye, Gaba, and beyond.

Here, we foster a deep sense of belonging — every child and parent becomes part of our close-knit family. This spirit of togetherness defines who we are and shapes the nurturing environment we provide.
