About RIS

Fostering learning, growth, and lifelong success.

Redemption International School was established on September 23, 2019, beginning with ten students and six committed parents who recognized the need to close gaps in their children's development and sought a more effective approach to education. While international education had grown in popularity, many essential elements were still missing. RIS was founded to bridge these gaps and offer a well-rounded, future-ready learning experience.

We believe education is a shared partnership between the school and home, where parents play a vital role in nurturing character and supporting academic growth. To support this, RIS provides a rich, internationally-oriented environment that blends academic rigor with hands-on skill development. Alongside our core academic curricula, we provide multilingual education in German, French, Spanish, Mandarin and Kiswahili, alongside advanced STEM training in AI, robotics, coding, and programming to prepare students to thrive in a technology-driven world.

Why choose us?

Teaching children how to live not just how to make a living

At RIS, your child will benefit from small class sizes, personalized attention, and a unique flexibility of choices. We are proud to offer three distinct educational pathways: ACE, Cambridge, and CBC, all tailored to match your child's learning style and future goals. Conveniently located at the Munyonyo Roundabout with easy access via the Bypass and Entebbe Expressway, we proudly serve communities as far as Buziga, Kigo, Gaba, Makindye, Ntinda, Gayaza, and beyond.

Here, we foster a deep sense of belonging, where every child and parent becomes part of our close-knit family. This spirit of togetherness defines who we are and shapes the nurturing environment we provide.
