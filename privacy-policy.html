Privacy Policy

How Redemption International School collects, protects, and handles personal data.

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

At Redemption International School (RIS), located in Munyonyo, Kampala, Uganda, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our learners, parents, guardians, faculty, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and safeguard data in accordance with recognized data protection regulations and applicable laws in Uganda.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information through our website, application forms, or direct correspondence, including:

  • Parent / Guardian Details: Full name, telephone number, residential address, email address, relationship to the student, and emergency contact details.
  • Student Details: Full name, date of birth, previous academic background, medical or dietary notes relevant to school welfare, and grade level of interest.
  • Inquiry & Communication Data: Any message, question, or attachment submitted via our contact or inquiry forms or sent to our official email addresses.
  • Technical Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, pages visited, and general browsing statistics collected via standard server logs.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for legitimate educational and institutional purposes, which include:

  • Processing school admissions, student placement, and enrollment verification.
  • Communicating with parents regarding academic progress, school announcements, events, and emergency updates.
  • Fulfilling educational and administrative requirements under the ACE, Cambridge, and CBC curricula.
  • Maintaining student health, safety, and pastoral care records while on school premises.
  • Responding promptly to prospective family inquiries and scheduling campus tours.
  • Enhancing website functionality, security, and user experience.

4. Child & Student Data Protection

As an educational institution catering to minors, student data protection and child safeguarding are of paramount importance. We never sell, lease, or commercially exploit any personal data belonging to children or families. Photographs or video clips showcasing student activities are published only in line with parental media consent protocols.

5. Data Security and Confidentiality

We apply robust administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to shield personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or misuse. Access to student and family information is restricted strictly to authorized administrative and academic personnel who require it to perform their official duties.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not disclose personal information to outside parties except where necessary to administer academic curriculum assessments (e.g., examination bodies such as Cambridge International or curriculum accreditation boards), comply with statutory legal directives from regulatory authorities, or protect the vital health and safety interests of an individual.

7. Your Rights

Parents and guardians have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about their children, request updates or corrections to outdated records, or inquire about how their information is processed. Requests can be submitted to our administrative office at any time.
