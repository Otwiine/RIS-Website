Overview

Christian-Based, Individualized Learning

The Accelerated Christian Education (ACE) curriculum is a Bible-based, highly individualized K-12 learning system. Rather than keeping all students on the same page regardless of their comprehension, ACE allows each learner to progress at their own pace, ensuring complete mastery of a concept before moving forward.

Character building is woven directly into every subject, nurturing moral values, self-discipline, and a strong sense of personal responsibility. With its global recognition, ACE equips students to transition smoothly into top international universities while remaining firmly grounded in values.
